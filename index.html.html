🔬 The Science: Why Equestrian Leggings Cannot Be Cut 1:1 to Body Size

❌ Case A: Cutting 1:1 (71 cm Table Measure)

Result: Critical Functional Failure in Saddle.

If the waistband is cut flat at 35.5 cm (71 cm loop) to match the rider's exact waist:

  • Zero elastic tension holding the breeches up.
  • The pants slide down immediately when mounting the horse.
  • Excess fabric folds and creates painful pressure creases between rider and saddle.
  • Full-seat and inner-knee silicone grip cannot lock properly because the fabric sags.

✅ Case B: Conbello Engineered Fit (33 cm Table Measure)

Result: Zero-Defect Professional Riding Performance.

With our engineered -5.5 cm negative ease (33 cm flat 1/2 waist):

  • High-recovery elastane core activates balanced 360° compressive support.
  • High anatomical double-layer waistband stays anchored over the iliac crest.
  • Fabric remains 100% squat-proof without over-sheering (300–320 GSM interlock density).
  • 500-micron silicone grip stays flat, taut, and firm against saddle leather.
